## Design Direction

Chosen initial direction: soft natural minimalism, close to Takram / Japanese home organization editorial language.

Reason:
- The logo already uses home + leaf symbolism.
- The planning document asks for clean, ordered, warm, professional visual tone.
- Storage service needs trust and calm more than decorative visual impact.
